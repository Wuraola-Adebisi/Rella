YOUR INFORMATION, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the Rella website and use the current Rella workspace.

Last updated: 22 November 2026
In brief: The current Rella MVP stores workspace information in your browser using local storage. It does not currently provide user accounts, a Rella-operated cloud database or cross-device synchronisation. Your workspace entries are not sent to a Rella application server by the current product.

1. About this policy

Rella is a personal life-management workspace designed to help people organise tasks, plans, recurring payments, notes and other useful personal details. This policy applies to the public website and the browser-based product currently available at this domain.

2. Information you add to Rella

When you use the workspace, information you enter or change is saved in local storage in the browser on the device you are using. This may include tasks, calendar events, bill details, notes, saved memories, inbox entries and conversations with the in-app assistant.

The current MVP does not require you to create an account and does not send this workspace content to a Rella-operated application server. The assistant experience is simulated within the application and works with the records stored in your current browser workspace.

3. Where your information is stored

Workspace information remains in the browser storage for the browser profile and device where you entered it. It is not automatically available on another device or in another browser. Clearing browser site data, using a different browser profile, or resetting the workspace may remove locally stored information.

The workspace includes an export option so you can prepare a copy of your saved data. You are responsible for keeping that exported copy somewhere secure. Do not use a shared or public device for sensitive information.

4. Website hosting and third-party resources

The website is hosted using Vercel. Like other website hosting providers, the hosting provider may process technical connection information such as IP address, browser and device details, request information and security logs when a page is requested. That processing is governed by the provider's own policies.

Rella also loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive technical connection information. Their services are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current Rella MVP does not intentionally use advertising trackers or a Rella-operated analytics profile. The workspace uses browser local storage to retain your entries. Hosting or third-party resource providers may use their own technical processes; consult their policies for details.

6. How to remove your information

You can remove information from the workspace using the controls available in the app, reset the sample workspace, or clear site data for this website in your browser settings. Export any information you want to keep before resetting or clearing browser data. Removing local browser data does not remove technical records that may have been created independently by hosting or third-party providers.

7. Children's privacy

Rella is a general-purpose personal organisation tool and is not designed to collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information entered by a child, please remove the relevant local browser data from the device.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Rella's features and infrastructure change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised. If future versions introduce accounts, cloud storage, integrations, analytics or other data processing, this policy should be updated to explain those changes before those features are used.

9. Questions

If you have a privacy question, use the contact channel published on the Rella website when one is made available. Do not send passwords, financial account credentials or other highly sensitive information through general enquiries.
